Dwell
Building Blocks for Biblical Literacy

Slow down. Re-read.
Be transformed.

True transformation comes from the Holy Spirit. Dwell is simply a rhythm to slow down across five readings so Scripture can dwell in you richly. Like the tree planted by streams in Psalm 1, it helps you delight, take root, and listen. Practice one step or stack all five.

Begin with Discover
D Discover
Step One • Discover

Find the historical background.

  • Use a study Bible or trusted resource to set the scene:
    Author • Audience • Time • Purpose
  • Set aside outside resources to let the passage speak for itself. Read slowly and record what stands out to you.
